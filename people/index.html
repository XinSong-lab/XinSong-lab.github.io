    PI

    Prof. Song Xin
    Assistant Professor
    Department of Biomedical Engineering,
    City University of Hong Kong
    Email: xinsong@cityu.edu.hk
    Tel: +852 34429562
    Office: Yeung-P7709

    Prof. Xin Song is an Assistant Professor at City University of Hong Kong. He has been selected for a National Young Talent programme (国家级青年人才) in China and recognised as an Asia-Pacific U30 Outstanding Young Leader (亚太U30杰出青年领袖). He received his bachelor’s and master’s degrees from Sichuan University and his PhD from Imperial College London in 2023, and previously served as a Research Assistant Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. His research focuses on intelligent polymeric biomaterials and biorobotic systems for blood purification, targeted therapy, and vascular diseases. He is the principal investigator of several research projects, including the National Natural Science Foundation of China’s Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas) and the Hong Kong Research Grants Council’s Early Career Scheme. He has published over 40 papers, including 14 as first or corresponding author in journals such as Nature Biomedical Engineering, Nature Reviews Bioengineering, Science Advances, Cell Biomaterials, Advanced Materials (three papers), and The Innovation. His publications have received over 1,600 citations, with an h-index of 23. He holds seven granted invention patents, one of which has been transferred to industry. He serves as a founding editorial board member of Advanced Mechatronics and Precision Manufacturing, and as an early-career editorial board member of Nano-Micro Letters, The Innovation, Research, BME Frontiers, Exploration, and Biomedical Technology.
